Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you make an informed decision.

Why choose Flutter over React Native or Swift/Kotlin? +
Flutter renders directly via Impeller/Skia down to the GPU, guaranteeing identical 60/120 FPS performance and pixel-perfect UI fidelity across iOS, Android, and Web from a single codebase. This cuts development and maintenance costs in half without any sacrifice in native quality.
How long does an MVP build take? +
A standard investor-ready MVP typically takes between 3 to 6 weeks, depending on complexity. Because I utilize proven architectures with Supabase/Firebase and automated CI/CD templates, we avoid reinventing boilerplate and focus immediately on your core value proposition.
How do we handle communication and progress updates? +
You receive weekly sprint updates, live testable demo links, and direct communication via Slack, WhatsApp, or email. You will always know what is being built, what is completed, and what is next.
Will I own 100% of the code and intellectual property? +
Yes, absolutely. All repository code, database schemas, graphic assets, and deployment keys are transferred directly to your organization upon project completion. Zero vendor lock-in.
