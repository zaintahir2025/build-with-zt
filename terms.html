Last updated: October 16, 2026. Plain, founder-friendly terms establishing project execution standards and client ownership.

1. Engagement & Scope of Work

When you engage Build With ZT for custom software engineering (such as Flutter cross-platform applications, MVP sprints, performance tuning, or web platform builds), work is performed pursuant to an agreed technical specification and milestone roadmap.

2. 100% Intellectual Property Ownership

You retain complete, exclusive ownership of your product, source code, database schemas, creative brand assets, and custom logic. Upon completion of milestone payments, all intellectual property rights and repository access are transferred to your business with zero vendor lock-in.

3. Mutual Confidentiality

All proprietary business information, technical architecture, database credentials, and unreleased product roadmaps shared during our engagement are treated with strict confidentiality. Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) are gladly signed before technical onboarding.

4. Milestone Deliveries & Verification

Projects are delivered in iterative, testable stages. At each milestone, you receive working builds to verify against documented acceptance criteria before proceeding to the subsequent development sprint.

5. Post-Launch Warranty

Every production build is backed by a 30-day bug-fix warranty covering any deviations from the agreed specifications at zero additional cost to guarantee a rock-solid market launch.
